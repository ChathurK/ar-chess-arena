AR Chess Arena

Chess in augmented reality, entirely in the browser. Two modes, two different ways of anchoring a board to the real world.

Marker-based

Puzzle Mode

  • Point your camera at a printed marker.
  • A board will appear on it.
  • Solve the checkmate puzzle before you run out of moves.
 Markerless

Duel Mode

  • Place the board on a flat surface.
  • Play a full game against a friend on their own device.
  • Moves sync live in real time.

Before you start

  • Both modes need HTTPS and camera permission — browsers will not hand a page the camera over plain HTTP.
  • Puzzle Mode works on most phones. Duel Mode's board placement needs WebXR hit-testing, which today means Chrome on a recent Android device; it also offers a non-AR view so you can play on a laptop.
  • Puzzle Mode needs the marker — open it here to print it or show it on another screen.
  • In Duel Mode each player places their own board in their own room. The boards are not in a shared physical space; the game is what is shared.
