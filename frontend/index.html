AR Chess Arena

Chess in augmented reality, entirely in the browser. Two modes, two different ways of anchoring a board to the real world.

Marker-based

Puzzle Mode

  • Point your camera at a printed marker.
  • A board will appear on it.
  • Solve the checkmate puzzle before you run out of moves.
Markerless

Duel Mode

  • Place the board on a flat surface.
  • Play a full game against a friend on their own device.
  • Moves sync live in real time.
