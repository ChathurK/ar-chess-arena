## What still has to be done

A CC-BY licence is satisfied by crediting the author wherever the work is
used, not by recording it here. Before submitting:

1. Credit the author in `README.md`, replacing the line stating that the
   models are self-authored and need no attribution.
2. Credit the author somewhere visible in the running application -- the
   landing page is the natural place.
3. Credit the author in the technical report, and update the section
   arguing that asset sourcing was a dead end.

The generated set in `../models/` carries none of these obligations and
remains available as a fallback.
